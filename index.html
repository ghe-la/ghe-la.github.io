About rClone

rClone is a file synchronization and cloud storage management utility that connects directly to your Google Drive to help you back up, organize, and transfer files safely.

  • Google Sign-In & Drive Integration: Used strictly to authenticate your Google account and perform file operations you explicitly initiate.
  • Local & Direct Processing: Credentials and OAuth tokens are stored locally on your machine and are never transmitted to external third-party servers.
