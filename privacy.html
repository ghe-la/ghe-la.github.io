Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize rClone using Google OAuth, the application accesses only the Google account profile and Google Drive scopes that you explicitly approve on the Google consent screen.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your Google OAuth credentials and Drive permissions are used solely to authenticate your session and execute file synchronization, backup, or transfer commands initiated by you. We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data or files with any third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are stored locally within your configuration file on your own device. No user files or authentication tokens are stored on external developer servers.

4. Revoking Access and Data Deletion

You can revoke rClone's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page, and delete your local configuration file from your device. For any privacy inquiries, contact hemin.ghela@gmail.com.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hemin.ghela@gmail.com.
